Privacy policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

VendorTagPro is made by Pino Consulting LLC ("we"). This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Your data stays on your phone

Your cards, items, prices, sales and settings are stored only on your device. We don't have servers, we don't collect or receive any of it, and the app has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. The only things the app sends anywhere are described below.

Market prices

To show market prices, the app sends a card's name, set and number to TCGdex (api.tcgdex.net), a free card-price service. Nothing about you, your prices or your sales is sent, and you can use the app without it (it then shows the prices it last saved).

Camera

The camera is used only to read the QR codes and barcodes you scan. Images are not saved or sent anywhere by the app. On Android, scanning uses the code scanner in Google Play services, which runs on the device; Google Play services may collect diagnostic information as described in Google's privacy policy. On iPhone, scanning uses Apple's built-in scanner.

Files

The app reads a file only when you choose one to import, and writes a file (sticker PDFs, sales exports) only where you choose to save it.

Links

Buttons such as eBay, PSA and Card Ladder open those websites in your browser. What happens there is covered by those sites' own privacy policies.

Backups

Your phone's own backup (Google or iCloud backup, if you have it turned on) may include the app's data, under your Google or Apple account. We can't see those backups.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored on the phone. Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If the app ever starts sending data off your phone, we will update this policy and say so in the app before it happens.
