Support

Questions, problems or ideas: email support@vendortagpro.com. Tell us your phone model and the app version (shown in your phone's app settings) if something isn't working.

Back up your sales after every show

Your data is stored only on your phone. At the end of each day, open Today's sales and tap Export all sales, then save the file somewhere safe (cloud storage or email it to yourself).

Getting your inventory in

Use Import cards for a card list (CSV), or Add item on the home screen for stocked items like magnets and prints.

Printing stickers

On the Stickers screen, pick what to print and tap Make PDF. Save the PDF, then print it from your label printer's app.

Deleting your data

Everything the app stores is on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes it. See the privacy policy.
